# Hero Ability Guide

Abilities unlock only at tier 6. There are no secondary abilities. Original tier-4 and tier-5 upgrades remain. Tier-6 abilities retain 125% strength and 20% shorter base waits. Damage below is before the 25% hero damage bonus and support bonuses. Tool Maker then applies its 25% damage reduction, for 93.75% of listed damage before support. Second Wind cannot shorten itself or another Second Wind.
