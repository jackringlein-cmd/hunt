The Skeleton Field Guide

The Hunt for the Necromancer · All 9 enemy types · Pictures from the current game

01Skeleton

The basic enemy. Walks toward the village with no special powers.

02Tiny Skeleton

Arrives in a tight group of ten. Each takes damage separately; movement effects affect the whole group. The circle shrinks as members die.

03Skeleton Runner

The fastest skeleton. Rushes toward the village, but has less health than a basic Skeleton.

04Shield Skeleton

Carries armor that reduces ordinary hits by 3 damage. Each hit still deals at least 1 damage.

05Skeleton Brute

A slow, tough skeleton with lots of health. Removes 5 villagers if it gets through.

06Bone Captain

Speeds up nearby enemies by 20%. Two Captains can combine their boosts for 40% extra speed.

07Bone Shaman

Every 5 seconds, gives a nearby monster a shield that absorbs 40 damage.

08Skeleton Giant

A huge, tough enemy. Blocks its first 7 harmful effects; after that, fear lasts 30% less time. Removes 20 villagers if it gets through.

09Bone Dragon

The final boss on wave 100. Blocks its first 15 harmful effects; after that, fear lasts 65% less time. Removes 50 villagers if it gets through.

Harmful effects include slows, stuns, freezes, fear, and poison. Blocking an effect does not block ordinary damage.
