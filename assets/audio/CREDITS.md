skull-scream-source.mp3 is the original recording.
skull-scream.wav is a 1.4-second mono excerpt starting at 1.6 seconds, with a 20ms fade-in and 100ms fade-out. Playback blends a descending recorded shriek (65%) with a pitched-down, low-pass creature-throat layer (35%). Both use a shared envelope peaking at gain 0.55. Other music and effects duck to 25% during the screech, then fade back over 0.25 seconds. The master volume still controls every sound. Layers end within the 1.4-second animation. No synthesized tone is layered onto this scream.
Rebuild with node scripts/prepare-skull-scream.cjs while the local game server is running.
